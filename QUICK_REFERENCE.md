## 💡 Tips

- **Multiple Jump Buttons**: Use A, RT, or LT - whichever feels best!
- **Analog Movement**: In platformer levels, gently tilt the stick for precise movement
- **Practice Mode**: Use Y to remove checkpoints, X to restart from current checkpoint
- **Navigation**: D-pad works everywhere as a backup to analog stick

---

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

**Controller not responding?**
- Make sure it's paired via Bluetooth Settings
- Check that the controller is MFi-certified
- Restart GD after connecting

**Button textures not showing?**
- Text hints will appear instead at bottom of screen
- Full button icon support may require future updates

**Analog stick drifting?**
- There's a 20% deadzone - slight movements are ignored
- If it persists, recalibrate your controller in iOS Settings
