# MFI Controller Support

Play Geometry Dash on iOS with your favorite game controller!

## 🎮 What is this?

This mod enables full support for MFi (Made for iPhone/iPad/iPod) game controllers in Geometry Dash on iOS. Simply connect your Xbox, PlayStation, or any other MFi-certified controller and start playing!

## ✨ Features

- **Automatic Detection**: Controllers are detected and connected automatically
- **Hot-Plugging**: Connect or disconnect controllers anytime, even during gameplay
- **Low Latency**: Direct integration with Apple's GameController framework for responsive controls
- **Universal Support**: Works with all MFi-certified controllers

## 🎯 Supported Controllers

- Xbox Wireless Controller
- PlayStation DualShock 4 & DualSense
- SteelSeries Nimbus & Stratus
- Backbone One
- Razer Kishi
- Any MFi-certified game controller

## 🕹️ Controls

### In-Game
- **A / Right Trigger / D-pad Up**: Jump
- **B / Left Trigger**: Secondary action

### Menus
- **A**: Select
- **B**: Back
- **D-pad/Thumbsticks**: Navigate

## 📝 How to Use

1. Enable the mod in Geode
2. Restart Geometry Dash
3. Connect your MFi controller via Bluetooth
4. Start playing!

The mod will automatically detect your controller when it connects and show a message in the logs.

## ⚠️ Requirements

- iOS 13.0 or later
- MFi-certified game controller
- Geometry Dash 2.2074

## 💡 Tips

- Make sure your controller is fully charged for best performance
- Keep your controller firmware up to date
- If the controller doesn't respond, try reconnecting it
